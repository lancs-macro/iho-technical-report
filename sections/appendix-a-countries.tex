\section{Country Data Sources}
\label{app:countries}

Table~\ref{tab:country-sources} lists the 25 countries in the IHPD and their
primary house price index source. Full source documentation, including
descriptions of seasonal adjustment and backfill methods applied to each
country series, is available in the Dallas Fed Appendix~A at
\url{https://www.dallasfed.org/~/media/documents/research/international/houseprice/appendixA.pdf}
and in \citet{mack2011}.
