\section{Coverage and Country Documentation}
\label{app:countries}

\subsection*{Where the country-level documentation lives}

This report does not reproduce the national source documentation for the
IHPD. That documentation is maintained by the team that constructs the
database, is revised whenever a source changes, and would go stale here. It
is available in two places:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \citet{mack2011}, Section~4, which treats each economy in turn,
        giving the reference series, the historical series used to extend it,
        the source of the PDI and working-age population data, the PCE
        deflator used, and the specific frequency-conversion, splicing and
        adjustment decisions applied. Appendices~A and~B of the same paper
        tabulate the national sources and summarise the reported frequency,
        units, geographic coverage, property type, property vintage and
        priced unit of each national series.

  \item The Dallas Fed's own country-source appendix, maintained alongside
        the data at
        \url{https://www.dallasfed.org/~/media/documents/research/international/houseprice/appendixA.pdf}.
\end{itemize}

\noindent Readers needing to know what a particular country's series
actually measures --- which is necessary before drawing conclusions from a
single-country result --- should go to those sources rather than to this
report.
