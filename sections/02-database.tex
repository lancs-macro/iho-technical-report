\section{The International House Price Database}
\label{sec:database}

\subsection{Overview}

The International House Price Database (IHPD) is maintained by the
Globalization Institute of the Federal Reserve Bank of Dallas. It provides
quarterly, seasonally adjusted, cross-country comparable house price and
income series for 25 economies, beginning in 1975\,Q1. The construction
methodology is described in detail in \citet{mack2011}.

\subsection{Variables}

Each country series contains four quarterly time series, defined in
Table~\ref{tab:variables}.

\begin{table}[ht]
  \centering
  \caption{IHPD variable definitions}
  \label{tab:variables}
  \begin{tabular}{lp{10cm}}
    \toprule
    Variable & Description \\
    \midrule
    HPI  & Nominal house price index. Country-specific source index selected
           for consistency with the U.S.\ FHFA quarterly index for existing
           single-family homes. \\[4pt]
    RHPI & Real house price index. HPI deflated by the country's personal
           consumption expenditure (PCE) deflator, rebased to
           2005\,=\,100. \\[4pt]
    PDI  & Nominal personal disposable income per capita (PDI divided by
           working-age population), indexed to 2005\,=\,100. \\[4pt]
    RPDI & Real personal disposable income per capita. PDI deflated by the
           country's PCE deflator, indexed to 2005\,=\,100. \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
\end{table}

\subsection{Construction Methodology}

\paragraph{Base year.}
All indices are rebased to 2005\,=\,100 to ensure cross-country
comparability.

\paragraph{Deflator.}
The personal consumption expenditure (PCE) deflator is used to construct
real series, consistent with U.S.\ national accounts methodology.

\paragraph{Per capita income.}
Personal disposable income is expressed per working-age capita to control
for demographic variation across countries and over time.

\paragraph{Seasonal adjustment.}
An unobserved components time series model is estimated over the full
available sample for each country's nominal house price index.

\paragraph{Source selection.}
For each country, the house price series is selected to maximise
consistency with the U.S.\ Federal Housing Finance Agency (FHFA) quarterly
index for existing single-family homes. Where data do not extend back to
1975\,Q1, historical sources are used to backfill the series.

\paragraph{Weighted aggregates.}
Cross-country aggregates use purchasing-power-parity (PPP) adjusted GDP
shares from 2005 as weights.

\subsection{Update Schedule}

The database is updated quarterly. The most recent release covers
2025\,Q4. Data are available at
\url{https://www.dallasfed.org/research/international/houseprice}.

\subsection{Programmatic Access via \texttt{ihpdr}}

The \texttt{ihpdr} \textsf{R} package \citep{ihpdr} provides direct download
of IHPD series, GSADF test statistics, and BSADF sequences.

\begin{lstlisting}[caption={Downloading IHPD data with \texttt{ihpdr}}]
install.packages("ihpdr")
library(ihpdr)

raw   <- hpdr_raw()    # HPI, RHPI, PDI, RPDI for all countries
sadf  <- hpdr_sadf()   # SADF/GSADF statistics
bsadf <- hpdr_bsadf()  # BSADF sequences for date-stamping
\end{lstlisting}

The package is available on CRAN
(\url{https://cran.r-project.org/web/packages/ihpdr/}) and GitHub
(\url{https://github.com/kvasilopoulos/ihpdr}).
