\section{The International House Price Database}
\label{sec:database}

\subsection{Overview}

The International House Price Database (IHPD) is maintained by the
Globalization Institute of the Federal Reserve Bank of Dallas and provides
quarterly, seasonally adjusted, cross-country comparable house price and
income series. The database was originally established with 19 economies
in \citet{mack2011} and has since been extended to its current 25-country
coverage. Series begin in 1975\,Q1 for most countries; where earlier data
are unavailable, historical sources are used to backfill the series as far
back as possible. The construction methodology is described in detail in
\citet{mack2011}; country-level source details are in
Appendix~\ref{app:countries}.

\subsection{Variables}

Each country series contains four quarterly time series, defined in
Table~\ref{tab:variables}.

\begin{table}[ht]
  \centering

  \label{tab:variables}
  \begin{tabular}{lp{10cm}}
    \toprule
    Variable & Description \\
    \midrule
    HPI  & Nominal house price index. Country-specific source index selected
           for consistency with the U.S.\ FHFA quarterly index for existing
           single-family homes. \\[4pt]
    RHPI & Real house price index. HPI deflated by the country's personal
           consumption expenditure (PCE) deflator, rebased to
           2005\,=\,100. \\[4pt]
    PDI  & Nominal personal disposable income per capita (PDI divided by
           working-age population), indexed to 2005\,=\,100. \\[4pt]

    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
\end{table}

\subsection{Construction Methodology}

\paragraph{Base year.}
All indices are rebased to 2005\,=\,100 to ensure cross-country
comparability.

\paragraph{Deflator.}
The personal consumption expenditure (PCE) deflator is used to construct
real series, consistent with U.S.\ national accounts methodology.

\paragraph{Per capita income.}
Personal disposable income is expressed per working-age capita to control
for demographic variation across countries and over time.

\paragraph{Seasonal adjustment.}
An unobserved components time series model is estimated over the full
available sample for each country's nominal house price index. Because the
model is re-estimated with each quarterly data release, seasonal factors
are not produced in real time but are instead revised retrospectively over
the entire history. This approach trades real-time availability for smoother
and more consistent seasonal adjustment across revisions.

\paragraph{Source selection.}
For each country, the house price series is selected to maximise
consistency with the U.S.\ Federal Housing Finance Agency (FHFA) quarterly
index for existing single-family homes, giving preference to repeat-sales
or hedonic indices that control for quality change. Where data do not extend
back to 1975\,Q1, historical sources are used to backfill the series.
Country-level source details are in Appendix~\ref{app:countries}.

\paragraph{Weighted aggregates.}
Cross-country aggregates use purchasing-power-parity (PPP) adjusted GDP
shares from 2005 as weights \citep{pavlidis2018}. Fixing the weights at
2005 ensures that aggregate dynamics reflect structural differences in
housing market size rather than cyclical movements in GDP or exchange rates.
