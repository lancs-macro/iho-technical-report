\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:introduction}

\subsection{Background}

Real-time monitoring of international housing markets requires a consistent,
long-span, and cross-country comparable dataset. Country-level house price
databases are often incomparable due to differences in index construction,
base years, deflation methods, and seasonal adjustment. The Dallas Fed
International House Price Database (IHPD) addresses this by providing a
harmonised set of quarterly house price and income series for 25 economies,
applying uniform construction methods across all countries
\citep{mack2011}.

\subsection{The International Housing Observatory}

The International Housing Observatory is a collaborative research initiative
established in 2019 by three partner institutions:

\begin{itemize}
  \item Federal Reserve Bank of Dallas, Globalization Institute (United States)
  \item Lancaster University Management School, Economics Department
        (United Kingdom)
  \item University of Alicante, Department of Fundamentals of Economic Analysis
        (Spain)
\end{itemize}

The Observatory team consists of Enrique Mart\'{i}nez-Garc\'{i}a, Themis Pavlidis,
and Ivan Paya (Directors); Kostas Vasilopoulos (Web Administrator); and Erik Andres
Escayola (Researcher).

The Observatory provides a publicly accessible, real-time monitoring platform
for international real estate markets at
\url{https://int.housing-observatory.com/}. Exuberance indicators are updated
quarterly following each new data release from the Dallas Fed.

\subsection{Purpose and Scope}

This report documents four interrelated components of the Observatory's
infrastructure:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Database} (Section~\ref{sec:database}) --- the Dallas Fed
        IHPD: country coverage, variable definitions, and construction
        methodology.

  \item \textbf{Data access} (Section~\ref{sec:data-access}) --- how the raw
        data are retrieved, the file format of each quarterly release, and
        programmatic access via the \texttt{ihpdr} \textsf{R} package
        \citep{ihpdr}.

  \item \textbf{Methodology} (Section~\ref{sec:methodology}) --- the GSADF
        test \citep{phillips2015a, phillips2015b} and PSY-IVX decomposition
        \citep{shi2021} used to detect and date-stamp housing market
        exuberance.

  \item \textbf{Web platform} (Section~\ref{sec:website}) --- the
        Observatory's interactive dashboard, quarterly monitoring reports,
        and companion tools.
\end{enumerate}
