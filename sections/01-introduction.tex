\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:introduction}

\subsection{Background}

Housing markets play a central role in macroeconomic stability. Episodes of
speculative exuberance --- where real house prices detach from economic
fundamentals --- have historically preceded major financial crises, most
notably the global housing boom of the early 2000s that collapsed in
2006--07 and precipitated the 2008--09 financial crisis
\citep{pavlidis2016}.

Monitoring such episodes in real time, across multiple countries
simultaneously, requires a consistent, long-span, and internationally
comparable dataset alongside robust statistical methodology. Existing
country-level databases are often incomparable across nations due to
differences in index construction, base years, deflation methods, and
seasonal adjustment procedures.

\subsection{The International Housing Observatory}

The International Housing Observatory is a collaborative research initiative
established in 2019 by three partner institutions:

\begin{itemize}
  \item Federal Reserve Bank of Dallas, Globalization Institute (United States)
  \item Lancaster University Management School, Economics Department
        (United Kingdom)
  \item University of Alicante, Department of Fundamentals of Economic Analysis
        (Spain)
\end{itemize}

The Observatory provides a publicly accessible, real-time monitoring platform
for international real estate markets at
\url{https://int.housing-observatory.com/}. Exuberance indicators are updated
quarterly following each new data release from the Dallas Fed.

\subsection{Purpose of This Report}

This technical report documents three interrelated components of the
Observatory's infrastructure:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Data infrastructure} --- the Dallas Fed International House
        Price Database (IHPD), including country coverage, variable
        definitions, construction methodology, and programmatic access via
        the \texttt{ihpdr} \textsf{R} package \citep{ihpdr}.

  \item \textbf{Econometric methodology} --- the GSADF test of
        \citet{phillips2015a, phillips2015b} and the PSY-IVX decomposition
        approach of \citet{shi2021}, both used to detect and date-stamp
        periods of housing market exuberance.

  \item \textbf{Web platform} --- the structure and outputs of the Observatory's
        dashboard, quarterly monitoring reports, and companion tools.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Scope}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Data:} 25 countries, 1975\,Q1 -- present, updated quarterly.
  \item \textbf{Variables:} Real and nominal house price indices and personal
        disposable income indices, rebased to 2005\,=\,100.
  \item \textbf{Methods:} Recursive right-tailed unit root tests for explosive
        dynamics; fundamental--non-fundamental price decomposition.
  \item \textbf{Platform:} Interactive dashboard and quarterly PDF reports.
\end{itemize}
